What is Outfitly?

Outfitly is an iPhone app for your wardrobe. Save photos of outfits you love, organize them into categories, mark favorites and plan what to wear each day of the week. Everything stays on your iPhone.

How do I add outfits?

Tap the plus tab and choose photos. You can pick several at once; each photo becomes an outfit.

How does background removal work?

Outfitly can remove the background from your photos on your iPhone, without uploading them. Turn it on or off in Profile → Background removal.

I don't get the weekly reminder

The reminder needs notification permission. Check Settings → Notifications → Outfitly and make sure notifications are allowed.

Is my wardrobe backed up?

Outfitly has no account or cloud sync. Your outfits are included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) and come back when you restore one.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Profile → Delete all data. This can't be undone. Deleting the app also deletes its data.

How do I change the app's language?

Outfitly is available in 50 languages and follows your iPhone's language. To use a different language just for Outfitly, open Settings → Apps → Outfitly → Language.

Privacy
