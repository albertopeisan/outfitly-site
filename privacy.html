Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

Outfitly is an iPhone app for saving outfit photos, organizing them into categories and planning what to wear. It's made by Alberto Peinado Santana ("I", "me"). The short version: your wardrobe never leaves your iPhone. Outfitly has no server, no account and no sync.

What stays on your iPhone

Your photos, outfits, favorites, categories, plans and settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. I never receive, see or store them. Background removal runs on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework.

Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, so Outfitly can't see your photo library; it only gets the photos you pick.

Like every app's data, Outfitly's data is included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). Those backups are handled by Apple and by you, not by me.

What leaves your iPhone: anonymous diagnostics

To find and fix crashes, the App Store version of Outfitly sends anonymous diagnostics to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, stored in the European Union. They contain:

  • crash reports and app hangs: stack traces and the app's state at the time;
  • performance data: how long the app takes to start and to load screens;
  • your device model, iOS version, the app version and which screens were open.

They don't contain your photos, outfits, category names, plans or any other content, and no name, email, account or user ID. The app sends no identifier that links reports to you, and IP addresses are not stored. Diagnostics are not used for tracking or advertising, are not sold and are not combined with other data. Sentry keeps them for a limited time and then deletes them.

Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is my legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

What Outfitly doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and no data sold or shared with data brokers.
  • No location, contacts or Photos library access.

Notifications

If you allow it, Outfitly schedules a weekly reminder on your iPhone to plan your outfits. It's a local notification: nothing is sent to a server. You can turn it off in Settings → Notifications → Outfitly.

Deleting your data

In Outfitly, go to Profile → Delete all data to erase everything in the app. Deleting the app also deletes all its data. Because I hold none of your content, there's nothing to delete on my side; diagnostics are anonymous and expire automatically.

Children

Outfitly isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
